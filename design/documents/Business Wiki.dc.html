01 · THE CONCEPT

A Havenly-style matching platform for architecture

ArchitectHire connects clients with licensed architects who deliver stamped, permit-ready drawings entirely remotely. It serves both residential work — renovations, additions, new custom homes — and commercial spaces — tenant improvements, office and retail fit-outs, restaurant build-outs — where permit complexity and repeat business are highest. Beyond full design, the platform surfaces the surrounding service ecosystem — consults, drafting, 3D visualization, scanning, engineering, and permit help — so a client can start with a $145 consult and scale up to a stamped set only when the project demands it.

02 · COVERAGE

Launching in all 52 states at once

We're starting out across all 52 US jurisdictions — the 50 states plus Washington, D.C. and Puerto Rico. The client questionnaire captures the project's state up front, and every match is filtered to architects actively licensed there. The jurisdiction database (below) is what makes nationwide launch viable: complexity is scored per city/county rather than hand-managed market by market.

03 · DIFFERENTIATORS

Two moats competitors can't easily copy

The deliverable is a legal document

Unlike interior-design platforms, the output is stamped drawings carrying the architect's professional liability — which must come from an architect licensed in the client's jurisdiction. That requirement is the barrier clients can't navigate alone.

The jurisdiction database

A proprietary data layer scoring every city and county on permit complexity — historic overlays, seismic / coastal / wildfire zones, drawing requirements — powering pricing, matching, and quality. A data asset competitors can't replicate quickly.

04 · THE CLIENT JOURNEY

Questionnaire → estimate → matches → direct hire

1

Questionnaire & instant estimate. Project type, square footage, location, scope and timeline return an instant, itemized price range — our clearest proof of expertise and the top of the funnel. It sets expectations; it is not a quote.

2

2–3 best-fit matches. Pre-filtered on the hard requirements clients can't judge — state licensure, jurisdiction experience, residential vs commercial specialization, and capacity. Capped at 2–3 to avoid decision paralysis.

3

Scope on-platform. Chat or video with matches (doubling as the scoping call). Contact details open once the client picks an architect to hire; repeat clients can request a specific architect.

4

Introduce, then they hire. The client picks an architect and hires them directly, at that architect's own rate. We take nothing from the project — the introduction is the product — and the work runs on a tracked timeline.

05 · PRICING MODEL

The estimate is a range, not a quote

We no longer set prices. The estimate is a typical range built from real market data — proof we understand the work — and each professional sets their own rate from there, fixed or hourly, their call. We are never party to the payment.

PRODUCTIZED SERVICE TIERS

ACQUISITION
The typical-range estimator

Engine-generated from project type, square footage on a declining rate curve, modular add-ons (structural, MEP, 3D viz, energy calcs) and a jurisdiction complexity multiplier. It returns a typical range, not a binding quote — the professional sets their actual price. Our best acquisition asset and the sharpest proof of expertise.

DIRECT
How professionals price

Professionals set their own rates — fixed or hourly — and contract and invoice the owner directly, off-platform. We are never in the transaction and take no cut of any project. In short: we make the introduction, they run the deal.

06 · HOW IT MAKES MONEY

Flat subscriptions now, construction referrals later

A flat monthly subscription paid by professionals — $79 (Studio), $299 (Practice) and $699 (Firm), tiered on project ceiling, metro coverage and active pursuits. Revenue is fully decoupled from project delivery: we earn the same whether a set ships on time or not, and carry none of the liability of standing inside the transaction.

THE LONG-TERM PRIZE

Contractor and supplier referrals. Drawings are only 5–10% of project cost, so a 2–5% referral fee on construction can exceed the design revenue — making stamped drawings the acquisition product for the far larger downstream transaction. Commercial clients amplify this: they build repeatedly and refer at higher volumes.
